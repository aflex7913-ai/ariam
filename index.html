✨ My Dearest Love ✨

Sometimes I wonder how my life would've been if I had never met you...

And honestly...

I don't even want to imagine it.

Because before you, life was simply passing by...

But after you, life finally began. ❤️

The day you entered my world, everything changed forever.

The sky looked brighter.
The nights became more peaceful.
Music sounded sweeter.
And my heart finally discovered what true happiness feels like.

April 22... wasn't just another date on the calendar.

It became the day my lonely heart finally found its home. ❤️

Since that day, every single heartbeat of mine has whispered only one name...

Yours.

Every morning, I wake up grateful because somewhere in this world... you exist.

Every night, before closing my eyes, my last thought is always you. 🌙❤️

Distance may separate our hands...
but it will never separate our hearts.

No number of kilometres, no country, no border, no ocean, could ever make me love you any less.

Because love like ours doesn't live in distance...
it lives inside the heart. ❤️

If one day the stars disappeared...
I'd still find my way, because your smile shines brighter than every star in the universe. ✨

If time ever stopped, I wouldn't be afraid...

As long as I'm standing beside you, every second becomes forever.

They say people search their whole lives for someone who understands them.

I never have to search again.

Because I found you. ❤️

You are... my peace.
My comfort.
My happiness.
My safe place.
My favourite notification.
My biggest blessing.
And the most beautiful chapter my life has ever written. 💖

Whenever I smile... there's a little part of you inside that smile.

Whenever I laugh... it's because you've taught my heart how happiness feels.

Whenever I dream... you're always there. ❤️

If I had only one wish, I wouldn't ask for money, or success, or fame.

I'd simply ask... to hold your hand for the rest of my life. 🤍

If life gave me one thousand chances...

I'd choose you one thousand times.

If there were a million universes...

I'd search every single one, until I found you again... and fell in love with you... all over again. ❤️

Again.
Again.
Again.
Forever.

Thank you... for walking into my life.
Thank you... for loving me.
Thank you... for believing in me.
Thank you... for making ordinary days feel like beautiful memories. 🥹❤️

No matter what happens tomorrow...
No matter where life takes us...
No matter how difficult life becomes...

One thing will never change...

I will always love you. With all my heart. With every heartbeat. With every breath. For every tomorrow. Forever. ❤️

Happy Girlfriend's Day, My Beautiful Princess. 👑❤️

And if anyone ever asks me... "What is the most beautiful thing you've ever found?"

I'll smile... look into your eyes... and simply answer...

**"Her."** ❤️

Forever & Always...
Yours,
❤️ Logan ❤️
